# Strategic Thinking – Sep 2026
## Project Title
    A Predictive Analytics Solution for Identifying Depression Risk Among Older Adults
    
## Project Overview
The aims of this project are to examine the factors associated with depression in older adults and use cross sectional data collected at a single point in time. Using predictive analytics I aim to identify patterns and groups more likely to be in a high risk group in relation to depressive disorders. This analysis will be used to support evidence based screening, prioritisation and resource allocation.

## Core structure of the project
Organisation > Business goal > Business Problem > Business impact > Data > Data Science > Business Decision
- Who : Healthcare/community-care organisations
- When : Early-stage risk assessment
- What : Difficulty identifying older adults who may be at increased risk of depression early (Identify risk early ได้ยาก)
- Why:
    - Support earlier identification and prioritisation of older adults who may be at increased risk of depression (Business Goal)
    - Prioritizing assessment, support, mornitoring and allocating resources  more difficult (Business impact)
- Data(Where) : Demographic, health, social, and economic data (International data)
- How : Predictive Analytics identify people who may be at increased risk
